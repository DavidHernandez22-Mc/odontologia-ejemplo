Clínica Dental Chicó

Before we touch a tooth, you get a fixed, written quote. What you sign is what you pay.

Smile design

A smile planned around your face, your proportions and the way you laugh. You try it on before you decide.

We don't start from a catalog of perfect teeth. We study your photos, your bite and what you would like to change, then combine only the treatments your case actually needs. The goal is for you to look like yourself, with teeth that look healthy and natural.

What it can include

Porcelain veneers
Thin shells that change the shape, size or color of your front teeth. Before we make them, we show them to you in wax.
In-office whitening
Lightens the shade of your own teeth. It is often the first step, and sometimes the only one.
Cosmetic bonding
Fixes chipped edges, small gaps or dark fillings in a single visit.
Clear aligners
If your teeth need to move, aligning them first can spare them from being reshaped later.

Each plan combines only what your case needs. It might be a whitening session, or treatment in several stages.

How we work

  1. Assessment

    Photos, X-rays and a conversation about what you would like to change — and what you wouldn't.

  2. Design and trial

    We design your new smile and try it in your mouth, in wax, so you can see it before we touch a tooth.

  3. Treatment

    In stages and in one-hour appointments, with the plan and price in writing from day one.

  4. Follow-up

    Check-ups to see how your mouth adapts and how to care for the result at home.

What we aim for

  • Proportion. Teeth that suit your face, your lips and the way you smile.
  • Natural color. A white that looks healthy, not one you notice from across the room.
  • Keeping your teeth. When the result allows it, we choose the option that removes the least tooth structure.
  • Informed decisions. You see the proposal, you know the price, and you decide without pressure.

Every mouth is different and results vary from case to case. At your assessment we will tell you honestly what is possible and what isn't.

Dental implants

A titanium root that replaces a missing tooth and supports a porcelain crown.

The implant is placed in the bone during a short, planned procedure. Once the bone has bonded with it, a crown made to fit your mouth is attached on top. The neighboring teeth don't need to be reshaped.

  1. Assessment

    We review your general health, your gums and the bone in the area with X-rays, and tell you whether an implant is a good option for you.

  2. Planning

    We plan the implant position on a computer before surgery, and give you the plan and price in writing.

  3. Placement

    Under local anesthesia, in one appointment. Most patients return to their routine the next day, with clear instructions for the first few days.

  4. Restoration

    Once the implant is stable, we take impressions and place your final porcelain crown.

  5. Check-ups

    Regular reviews and professional cleanings to look after the implant and the gum around it.

Typical timeline

As a guide, the final crown is usually placed 3 to 6 months after the implant — the time the bone needs to integrate it. Your plan will give you your own timeline, which may be longer if you need a bone graft.

What can change the plan

  • The amount and quality of bone in the area.
  • The health of your gums.
  • Whether you smoke.
  • Conditions such as uncontrolled diabetes.
  • How many teeth need replacing.

What an implant can give you

  • Confident chewing. The crown rests on bone, not on the gum.
  • Untouched neighbors. Unlike a bridge, the teeth on either side don't need to be filed down.
  • Helps preserve bone. The implant stimulates the bone in the area, which tends to shrink when a tooth is missing.
  • Looks like a tooth. The crown is made to match the color and shape of your teeth.

Like any treatment, an implant needs good home care and regular check-ups. How long it lasts depends on that care and on your general health.

Questions

Does it hurt? Hardly ever. First we apply a gel that numbs the gum, then the anesthetic is delivered drop by drop by a computer-controlled device, which is what avoids the sharp sting.

What if I panic in the chair? Raise your hand and we stop, no questions asked. You can bring headphones, ask for breaks and, for longer procedures, choose conscious sedation.

Do you work with insurance? We work on a reimbursement basis: we give you the invoice and the documents your insurer asks for. We are not part of the Colombian public health system (EPS).

How long is an appointment? One hour. If the treatment needs less, the extra time is for your questions.

Do you see children? From age 4. A child's first visit is just to get to know the room and count teeth.

Can I pay in installments? Yes. You pay for each appointment when it ends, and longer treatments can be split into up to 12 interest-free installments on a credit card.

Do you handle emergencies? Monday to Saturday we keep two slots a day for pain. Message us on WhatsApp before 10 a.m.

What if I can't make it? Let us know 24 hours ahead and we'll move your appointment at no cost. We give that slot to an emergency.

Do aligners work for every case? For most. At your assessment we show you the simulation, and if your case is better suited to braces, we'll tell you.

How often should I come back? Every six months for a cleaning and check-up. We'll message you when it's time.

Is there parking? Yes, in the building's basement, with two hours free. You can also walk from the Calle 93 station.

I'm coming from abroad — how many days should I stay? It depends on the treatment. Whitening or bonding can be done in a few days; veneers need several appointments; implants usually require two trips a few months apart. We give you an estimate before you travel and confirm it at your assessment.

Can I know the cost before I travel? We can give you a guide range based on the information you send us. The fixed price is given in writing after your in-person assessment, because only then can we confirm the diagnosis.

What about follow-up once I'm home? We give you your plan, your X-rays and written recommendations so your local dentist can follow up. For any questions, we stay in touch on WhatsApp.
